Support

Questions, bugs, purchases: write to stefano@gamedevhub.co.th. We answer in English, usually within two working days.

Is every room really solvable?

Yes. Every room is generated and then solved by a computer solver before you see it. If a hint says the room is now unsolvable, a move you made closed the last way out: tap RESET and try again. That hint is free.

How do hints work?

A hint shows one correct next move, worked out by the same solver. It costs one hint token. You get free tokens every day (up to a limit), from awards, by watching an optional ad, or from the shop.

I bought something and don't have it

Open SHOP or SETTINGS and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with. Hint tokens and streak freezes can't be restored once used. If something is still missing, email us with the date of purchase. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

The daily room

A new daily room starts at midnight UTC, the same for every player. Retry as often as you want; only a solve counts toward your streak. A streak freeze is used automatically if you miss exactly one day.

Flashing, motion and sound

SETTINGS has Reduce Flashing & Shaking (dims and slows screen flashes, and removes camera shake and decorative motion), and separate switches and volumes for music, effects and the heartbeat, plus Mute All.

Ads

Ads between rooms appear only after a room you cleared, never right after a fail, and never in your first session. Remove Ads stops them. To change ad tracking, use iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. More in the privacy policy.
