Privacy Policy

99 Rooms of Madness for iPhone · Effective 15 November 2026

99 Rooms of Madness ("the game") is published by Stefano Bianchi, trading as Game Dev Hub ("we"). This policy explains what data the game handles and why. The short version: we run no servers and collect nothing ourselves. The advertising partner described below collects some data to show ads.

Data that stays on your device

  • Progress and settings: rooms cleared, hint tokens, streak, awards, statistics, owned items, and your sound, haptics and accessibility choices.
  • A local event log (for example "room cleared", "hint used"), kept to a small size on your device only.

None of this is sent to us. Deleting the game deletes it. Settings → Reset Progress clears progress at any time.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. The game only records which items you own, on your device, and asks Apple to restore them when you tap Restore Purchases.

Advertising (AppLovin MAX)

The game shows ads through AppLovin MAX. To deliver ads, limit how often you see them, measure them and prevent fraud, AppLovin may collect:

  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version);
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • ad interactions (which ads were shown or tapped);
  • crash and performance data of the ad software;
  • the advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when iOS asks. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

In regions where the GDPR applies (such as the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom) the game never gives consent for personalised advertising on your behalf, so ads there are not personalised.

AppLovin's privacy policy: applovin.com/privacy. If you buy Remove Ads, interstitial ads stop and optional rewarded ads are replaced by free claims.

Children

The game is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has used the game and you want data removed, contact us and we will help, including by asking AppLovin to delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, or to object to its use. Because we hold no data about you, requests about advertising data go to AppLovin; we will forward any request you send us. You can also limit ad tracking in iOS Settings at any time.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
